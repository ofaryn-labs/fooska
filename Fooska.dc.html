The loop

Three taps from hearing it to owning it.

1 · Capture

Hear a phrase you love? Tap record or type it in — before it slips away. Fooska grabs it in seconds.

2 · Understand

AI instantly fills in the meaning, a natural example sentence, and pronunciation — no dictionary hunting.

3 · Remember

Smart spaced practice resurfaces each expression right before you'd forget it — so it sticks for good.

Capture

Grab it the second you hear it.

Say it out loud or type it down. Fooska listens, cleans it up, and enriches it with meaning and context — so a phrase from tonight's show is a study card before the credits roll.

  • ✓ Voice capture with live transcription
  • ✓ Auto meaning, example & pronunciation

Practice

Practice that actually sticks.

Every day Fooska builds a bite-sized mix from exactly what's due — flashcards, listen-and-repeat, and fill-in-the-blank. No endless decks, no busywork. Just the words about to slip.

  • ✓ A fresh smart mix, generated daily
  • ✓ Flashcards, fill-in-the-blank and listen & repeat
  • ✓ Two-minute sessions that fit any day
Progress

Watch it compound.

Streaks, a consistency heatmap, and a mastery breakdown turn steady effort into something you can see growing.

Library

Your personal phrasebook.

Pronunciation

Say it out loud. Get a score.

Listen to the expression, slow it down if you need to, then record yourself saying it. Fooska scores accuracy, fluency and completeness — and tells you exactly which word tripped you up.

  • ✓ Word-by-word feedback, not just a number
  • ✓ Half-speed playback for the tricky ones
  • ✓ Phonetic transcription on every expression
